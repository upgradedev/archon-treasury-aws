# Archon Treasury (powered by Kerdon), submission description draft

Archon Treasury recalculates Airwallex FX and supplier payouts when a deposit lands, invalidating stale approvals so cross-border teams never breach their cash reserve floor.

Elena Vance runs finance and operations at a 14-person cross-border hardware studio. She has no full-time controller. Five supplier payouts in USD, EUR, and GBP fall due inside 72 hours, and client deposits land late. Today she juggles three spreadsheet tabs before she dares to approve a wire batch, because the balance she approved against this morning is not the balance she has tonight.

Archon Treasury puts the approval on the card she already opens. Every approval binds the amount in integer cents, the currency, the counterparty, the locked FX quote, and a SHA-256 hash of the exact evidence behind the decision. When a signed Airwallex deposit webhook lands, the engine recalculates on its own. Approvals whose evidence moved are invalidated in that instant. Approvals whose evidence did not move stay valid. A payment can execute only through a separate Writer gate that re-verifies the digest and the reserve floor, so a stale approval physically cannot pay.

Remove Airwallex and the product stops: no live multi-currency balances, no locked FX quotes, no idempotency-guarded transfers, no deposit webhook to trigger recalculation. The engine itself never guesses. Twenty-one deterministic completeness rules name what is missing: a bank debit with no supplier invoice, a client deposit never booked, a duplicate bill, a payout that would breach the floor. A model may parse an invoice and explain an exception. Only deterministic code decides.

On the frozen 74-case benchmark in this repo (62 completeness cases, 12 invalidation cases, labelled before any run), the deterministic engine scores 74 of 74. A naive one-pass amount matcher coded in the same script scores 24 of 74. A hosted Claude model alone, one unconstrained pass over the same 62 rule cases through Amazon Bedrock, scores 28 of 62. Run `python scripts/run_benchmark.py` and `python scripts/run_llm_benchmark.py` and check every number; both scripts print their method. One hundred and four automated tests pass with zero installs: `python -m unittest discover -s tests`. Line coverage of the pure core is 97 percent, measured by `python scripts/coverage_check.py`. The live page audits at zero axe-core violations at phone and desktop widths.

Approvals speak. Each one issues a short spoken receipt sealed with a Merkle root, readable by screen readers and refreshable Braille displays, with amounts in major units and cents side by side so the number Elena hears is the number the bank boundary receives. Three autonomous sub-agents pay per API call in USDZ on Metal under one governed budget: integer weights, hard call caps derived from live prices, and a refusal the moment a cap is hit.

Honest edges, labelled where they stand: the anonymous demo is live at https://pjlgavqdkhrcserxuqjz6icit40vbfxv.lambda-url.eu-west-1.on.aws/ and runs synthetic data on a fake Airwallex transport, which it says on its page (live sandbox wiring ships behind the same port with sandbox credentials, not yet connected). What a judge runs there today is the real core: the same rules, digests, receipts, and refusals the sandbox-connected build executes.
